About

Research that survives contact with the real robot.

I am Qingda Hu, an M.S. student at Fudan University working on robotic manipulation, action representations, long-horizon memory, and scalable learning.

I care about complete research loops: a precise problem, an efficient method, reproducible evaluation, and a system that still works outside the training codebase. My experience spans model design, data collection, large-scale training, simulation, and deployment on physical robots.
